### Definitions

- **VI-RF-01** `f_RF`: frequency of the ICP antenna drive
- **VI-RF-02** `P_fwd`: RF power travelling toward the matching network/antenna, measured by a directional coupler at the declared RF load plane (plane schemas/interfaces/icp_neutralizer_icd_v1.json ICP-14 (A9-03 ICD))
- **VI-RF-03** `P_refl`: RF power reflected back toward the generator at the same plane as P_fwd
- **VI-RF-04** `P_net`: P_fwd - P_refl at the declared load plane (includes matching-network and antenna ohmic loss downstream of the plane)
- **VI-RF-05** `P_abs`: eta_p x P_net with eta_p = R_p / (R_p + R_ant), R_p = R_total - R_ant (anchor Eq. (1) method); calorimetry of antenna/electrode heat as independent cross-check (row 72)
- **VI-RF-06** `R_ant`: antenna-circuit resistance from P_net / I_ant^2 with no plasma (gas off and gas on unlit, both recorded), with the module installed on H-1
- **VI-RF-07** `I_ant, R_total`: RMS antenna current and P_net / I_ant^2 during discharge at each operating point
- **VI-RF-08** `P_RF,DC`: electrical power drawn by the RF source (generator + matching control) at its DC input; enters P_bus through its own bus slot
- **VI-RF-09** `eta_RF`: P_net / P_RF,DC at each operating point (lab generator is not flight-representative; flight value PENDING)
- **VI-RF-10** `mode`: identification of capacitive (E) vs inductive (H) coupling at each point, and the net power / pressure of any E-H transition or hysteresis
- **VI-RF-11** `pickup`: RF-induced error on I_d, V_d, thrust-stand output and probe signals with the ICP energized and Hall off / on
- **VI-EX-01** `I_e,ICP`: net electron current leaving the ICP plasma toward the Hall anode and the beam, from the current balance of the module terminals (collector + body + any bias return) with the discharge supply isolated
- **VI-EX-02** `I_e,sat(P_net, mdot, V_coupling)`: maximum extractable electron current versus net RF power and gas flow at a registered coupling-voltage limit (saturation behaviour), measured with the Hall head as the electron sink
- **VI-EX-03** `V_coll`: potential of the ICP ion-collecting electrode with respect to the declared reference (cathode-common / facility ground, PENDING docs/interfaces/icp_neutralizer/ (A9-03 ICD))
- **VI-EX-04** `I_coll, V_bias, P_coll`: ion current collected by the electrode and the electrical power of its bias supply (if the validated circuit needs one)
- **VI-EX-05** `V_body`: floating potential of the ICP body (kept floating unless the validated circuit requires otherwise)
- **VI-EX-06** `V_cg`: potential of the electron-source reference (C1 cathode-common or ICP reference) with respect to facility ground, in both configurations at identical H-1 settings
- **VI-EX-07** `M_neut`: PROPOSED form (OQ-VI-02): electron-current capacity of the electron source at the registered coupling-voltage limit (VI-EX-02) relative to the measured Hall current demand at the same point (VI-HD-01); the numeric margin is frozen only at LOCK-2 (rows 18, 19)
- **VI-EX-08** `neut`: current balance of the plume (beam ion current vs electron current into the beam) and absence of net charging with the supplies isolated; plus far-field ion energy with anode-level maximum
- **VI-EX-09** `C_e`: (P_RF,DC + P_coll + ICP housekeeping) / I_e,ICP for the ICP; (heater + keeper + flow-control power) / I_emit for C1; both on the A9-02 bus boundary
- **VI-EX-10** `E_i,coll`: energy of ions striking the collector: (ICP plasma potential - V_coll) for ICP ions and (V_A - V_coll) for Hall ions reaching it
- **VI-HD-01** `I_d(V_d, B, mdot)`: discharge current of H-1 at each registered V_d / coil-current / feed point, measured first with the C1 reference and then with the ICP neutralizer at identical settings
- **VI-HD-02** `V_A, V_A - V_ref`: anode potential vs facility ground and the voltage actually across anode and electron-source reference (series elements and sheath drops separated)
- **VI-HD-03** `T`: sustained thrust of the full H-1 + electron-source configuration on the torsional stand with matched shams
- **VI-HD-04** `IEDF, I_b`: RPA/RFEA and Faraday data at registered positions for both configurations
- **VI-HD-05** `delta B(z)`: change of the H-1 field map with the ICP (or C1) module installed and energized vs reference, and the H-1 sensitivity scan used to freeze the allowable tolerance
- **VI-HD-06** `B_ICP(z, r)`: magnetic field of H-1 inside the downstream ICP volume (the 'unmagnetized' ICP sits in the Hall fringe field)
- **VI-HD-07** `I_d(t)`: time-resolved I_d with each electron source (to ~60 MHz if feasible, else the declared bandwidth and transfer function), including 13.56 MHz harmonics
- **VI-GAS-01** `gas_ICP`: the gas that sustains the ICP: (a) Hall exhaust re-use only (anchor topology), (b) a dedicated Xe feed (booked in the Xe ledger, docs/budgets/xe_ledger/), or (c) a dedicated atmospheric feed; UNBOOKED today (A9 recorder flag row 46)
- **VI-GAS-02** `mdot_ICP`: mass flow of any dedicated ICP feed per species, booked as PHASE_TOTAL_FLOW (purge, ignition, operation, transition, fallback)
- **VI-GAS-03** `p_ICP`: pressure inside the ICP volume fed by the Hall exhaust (and any dedicated feed), at a declared tap
- **VI-GAS-04** `C_HE-ICP`: measured conductance / pressure ratio between the H-1 exit plane and the ICP volume (cold flow and hot)
- **VI-GAS-05** `p_b`: background pressure at the registered location per run, at the nominal and two elevated levels
- **VI-GAS-06** `mdot / I_e`: electron-source gas flow per extracted ampere (ICP: dedicated flow share; C1: cathode Xe flow)
- **VI-GAS-07** `mdot_s, P_feed, T_feed, x_s`: feed state at the valve outlet / anode-distributor inlet, identical in both configurations
- **VI-PB-01** `P_bus,ICP`: sum of every ICP-related load at the spacecraft-DC propulsion boundary: RF source, collector/bias supply, any assist magnet or active cooling, ICP housekeeping
- **VI-PB-02** `P_bus,C1`: heater, keeper (incl. pulsed ignition), cathode flow control and any filter/getter load of the C1 reference
- **VI-PB-03** `P_bus(t)`: time-resolved sum over all slots from cold start through steady state for each configuration
- **VI-PB-04** `B_alloc`: internal propulsion design allocation (~1.35 kW) inside which the ICP power must fit; the 1.35-1.5 kW margin is not consumed nominally
- **VI-SU-01** `ign_ICP`: ignition success per attempt, attempts, forward power, pressure and time-to-ignite of the ICP, with and without Hall flow
- **VI-SU-02** `ign_Hall|ICP`: Hall discharge ignition success, attempts and onset V_d with the ICP as the only electron source; confirm 'no Hall discharge without ICP electrons' as an engineering-only topology check
- **VI-SU-03** `N_restart, N_cycle`: restart success rate and accumulated on/off cycles per configuration, classified per the preregistration
- **VI-SU-04** `m_Xe,start`: C1 purge / preheat / ignition Xe booked as PHASE_TOTAL_FLOW, ignition dwell capped at 120 s with at most two retries in the preliminary protocol
- **VI-SU-05** `mdot_c`: C1 spot-mode steady flow found with a 0.005 mg/s step search and preregistered stopping criteria
- **VI-LF-01** `dm/dt, dh/dt`: mass loss and profile change of the ICP ion-collecting electrode per hour at registered operating points
- **VI-LF-02** `deposit`: deposit mass/thickness and its effect on RF coupling (eta_p drift) and on H-1 insulators, via witness coupons
- **VI-LF-03** `d eta_p / dt`: change of R_ant, R_total and eta_p with accumulated hours and cycles
- **VI-LF-04** `T_ICP, T_ant, T_RF`: temperatures of the ICP tube, antenna, matching network and RF source; margin >= 50 K below each validated continuous-use limit plus 20 % heat-load design margin
- **VI-LF-05** `L_req, N_req`: required operating hours and on/off cycles of the ICP neutralizer (C1 carries the 15,000 h cathode term; the ICP carries its own requirement)
- **VI-LF-06** `compat`: material response of ICP tube, antenna shielding and collector to O2-bearing plasma (NO_ATOMIC_O) and, separately, to atomic O in the dedicated programme
- **VI-MS-01** `m_ICP`: v0 dry allocation for the ICP neutralizer
- **VI-MS-02** `m_RF`: v0 dry allocation for RF generator and matching
