## 0. What this is

The owner pivoted the decisive-thrust question to one controlled experiment on one Hall accelerator H-1 and one cathode C-1 (HW-0 `hall_only`, HW-RF `rf_hall`, HW-ECR `ecr_hall`), with two separate decisions: the architecture ratio R_arch and an absolute thrust gate T_measured on the actual delivered feed (sustained >= 12 mN, the registered 25 mN capability, P_bus < 1.5 kW). This document says, for each measured quantity, what instrument principle, uncertainty and calibration are needed and which decision or validation quantity it serves. It chooses no product, claims no accuracy, names no preferred architecture and eliminates none.

Compliance:

- no Hall transport closure, screening candidate or withdrawn 0-D number is used; no retuning
- no architecture is named preferred and none is eliminated; this document defines measurements only
- P5 calibration nuisance (registration, coil shape, divergence reading, facility interpretation) is never an axis or a design variable here
- the feed state is an IF-A5 input identical across arms; Hall-closure uncertainty does not reach intake, compressor, gas chambers or valves
- every number carries unit, evidence class and source (computed by this script or pinned), else TBD with what it requires
- the compressor bus draw and the W1 feed test points are never filled

### 3.3 Absolute gates (PROPOSED rule)

PROPOSED targets I-U-ABS-T = 1 % and I-U-ABS-P = 1 % (both assumed, owner decision). REF-SNYDER2017 reports 0.6-2 % uncertainties for direct thrust measurement, so 1 % is inside the reported range but not a claim for any stand. Conditions on the absolute gates:

- **Boundary.** The power gate on `bus_power_boundary_v1` needs the compressor draw reconstructed from the upstream ICD (ABSENT_IN_LAB). Until then the lab result is PARTIAL_BOUNDARY: a lab-subset FAIL is informative, a lab-subset PASS is necessary but not sufficient.
- **Feed (I-FLOW-CONSERVATIVE, PROPOSED).** The gate counts only if the delivered anode flow is not above the registered W1 test-point flow with one-sided confidence (mdot_hat (1 + k u_mdot) <= mdot_registered), so flow uncertainty can only make the demonstration conservative. Flow uncertainty at a setpoint for a 1 % FS device: 0.1 of FS: 0.1 relative (1 sigma treated); 0.2 of FS: 0.05 relative (1 sigma treated); 0.5 of FS: 0.02 relative (1 sigma treated); 1.0 of FS: 0.01 relative (1 sigma treated). Orientation zero shift (bound, REF-SNYDER2017 0.4 % FS): 0.1 of FS: 0.04 relative (bound); 0.2 of FS: 0.02 relative (bound); 0.5 of FS: 0.008 relative (bound); 1.0 of FS: 0.004 relative (bound).
- **Facility (PROPOSED reporting rule).** T_measured is reported at the measured p_b together with the S5 p_b slope and the ingestion scale below; no ingestion model is applied to rescue a gate (P5-N2 lesson, lane 25 Sec. 8). If the measured p_b slope times the p_b change to zero exceeds the gate margin, the gate is FACILITY_CONDITIONAL.
- **Sustained.** 'Sustained' uses THR-EXTINCTION over an owner-set hold time; the stand's zero drift over that dwell is bounded only by the pre- and post-dwell zeros (REF-POLK2017) and must fit inside I-U-ABS-T.

## 5. Instruments

### INS-01. thrust stand (inverted-pendulum or torsional, null-type preferred)

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M1; **quantity:** thrust T
- **Principle:** displacement or null-force pendulum with end-to-end in-situ calibration under vacuum (REF-POLK2017); inverted pendulum: compact and sensitive but inclination- and thermally sensitive, needs active leveling; torsional: response independent of thruster mass, needs a larger chamber (REF-POLK2017). Because the HW-RF / HW-ECR modules change the mass on the stand between arms, a mass-independent response (torsional) or a mandatory in-situ re-calibration after every configuration change is required (this lane's reading of REF-POLK2017; stand choice is W3/owner). Example of a null-type inverted pendulum: REF-XU2009
- **References:** REF-POLK2017, REF-XU2009, REF-SNYDER2017
- **Required uncertainty:** per_reading_repeatability: n4 0.00498361 relative (1 sigma, per reading); absolute_at_12mN_n4 59.8034 uN (1 sigma, per reading); absolute_at_25mN_n4 124.59 uN (1 sigma, per reading)<br>installation_reproducibility: n4 0.00249181 ln-ratio (1 sigma, per installation); note shared with INS-02 through ln(T/P_bus); S1b re-mount series (K = 6) measures it<br>absolute_for_gate: 0.01 relative (1 sigma)<br>range: TBD - requires H-1 thrust range and mass on stand incl. the RF/ECR modules (W3 hardware definition, planned path TBD by fo_hardware_definition); the calibration span must cover the expected thrust range (REF-POLK2017), from the knee-scan extinction end to >= 25 mN
- **Calibration:**
  - S1a: >= 10 in-situ calibrations with known forces spanning the expected range before and >= 10 after each operating sequence (REF-POLK2017; lane 06 thrust_cal_before/after_min, PROPOSED)
  - zero with thruster power and flow off before and after every point visit (REF-POLK2017; lane 25 Sec. 7)
  - thermal shrouds / active cooling of critical stand components, low-conductivity thruster mount, thermocouples on the stand (INS-17) (REF-POLK2017)
  - active inclination control with an inclinometer for an inverted pendulum (REF-POLK2017)
  - tares: propellant lines (solid metal tubing), power cables, RF coax / waveguide crossing the stand characterised per configuration; magnetic tare across the Hall and ECR coil-current settings with the discharge off (REF-POLK2017; lane 06 applies the principle to pre-ionizer feed lines)
  - force standard traceability class: TBD - requires owner (package TBD 'traceable calibration class')
- **Feasibility:** AT_RISK: lane 25 needs 0.50 % per-reading repeatability (n = 4) and 0.25 % re-mount reproducibility of ln(T/P_bus); REF-SNYDER2017 reports 0.6-2 % uncertainties for direct thrust measurement (absolute, not repeatability, so not directly comparable). At 12 mN the n = 4 target is about 60 uN (1 sigma). Achievability TBD - requires S1b; if D0 fails the owner widens delta, raises n, or adopts OPTION-DIVERTER (removes G5).
- **Note:** sustained >= 12 mN (DQ-TABS) needs a long dwell: zero drift over the dwell is bounded only by the pre- and post-dwell zeros; the dwell length and its drift allowance are TBD - requires owner (hold time) and S1 thermal time constants

- **Principle:** Hall-probe gaussmeter on a positioning stage, every configuration (HW-0, HW-RF, HW-ECR; ECR magnet on/off), before and after the campaign, at the operating coil currents; coil currents metered on INS-02 (hall_magnet, ecr_magnet)
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires the W5 pre-registered B(z) comparison tolerance (fo_hall_validation_prereg_draft; path TBD by that workstream) and the H-1 design range (W3)
- **Calibration:**
  - S1a: probe calibration in a reference field; stage position calibration; room-temperature mapping (hot-state effects TBD, lane 25 Sec. 12)
- **Feasibility:** FEASIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE: magnetostatic; the P5 non-identifiability (CLAUDE.md) is the reason it is mandatory with coil currents

### INS-10. stability / extinction / ignition detection

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M4; **quantity:** per-dwell extinction flag, restarts, ignition success within timeout
- **Principle:** applies THR-EXTINCTION (lane 06, same functional form as P5-N2 rule O1: mean I_d < 0.05 I_ref AND >= 90 % of samples < 0.1 I_ref over the window) to INS-04 traces; ignition attempts per the package D-13 module
- **Required uncertainty:** floor: I-U-ID-FLOOR (PROPOSED)<br>window: TBD - requires ext_window from the S1 oscillation band (lane 06)
- **Calibration:**
  - validated on S1 traces with commanded shutdowns (known extinctions) before any score-bearing reading
- **Feasibility:** FEASIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE: a logic on INS-04 data; thresholds PROPOSED

### INS-11. residual gas analyser (RGA)

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M13; **quantity:** background and feed composition (O2 fraction, contaminants)
- **Principle:** quadrupole RGA on the chamber (and, if practical, a differentially pumped sample of the feed line)
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires a W5 decision on whether feed composition is a validation observable; RGA is qualitative unless calibrated on the mixture
- **Calibration:**
  - calibrate against known N2/O2 mixtures from the INS-05 flows
- **Feasibility:** OPTIONAL: no reference located by this lane; qualitative O2 background check

### INS-12. optical emission spectroscopy (OES)

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M13; **quantity:** emitting species in source / interstage / plume
- **Principle:** spectrometer with fibre view of the source and interstage
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires a W5 decision; qualitative only unless a collisional-radiative model is admitted
- **Calibration:**
  - wavelength and relative intensity calibration
- **Feasibility:** OPTIONAL: qualitative (lane 25 M13); no quantitative species fraction without a model this project has not admitted

### INS-13. ExB probe (ion species / charge-state current fractions)

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M7; **quantity:** N2+, N+, O2+, O+ (and multiply charged) current fractions
- **Principle:** Wien filter with an accelerating bias for low-energy interstage ions (REF-ROVEY2025 as cited by lane 25)
- **References:** REF-ROVEY2025
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires W5 pre-registered species-fraction tolerance; CEX correction uncertainty is dominated by the background neutral density from ion gauges, typically 10-20 % (REF-ROVEY2025 via lane 25)
- **Calibration:**
  - mass-resolution check on known ions; same analysis code for all arms (lane 06 DIAG-EXB)
- **Feasibility:** AT_RISK: at low energy the four air ions are not all resolved without an accelerating bias (REF-ROVEY2025 via lane 25)

### INS-14. retarding potential analyzer (RPA)

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M6; **quantity:** ion energy distribution (interstage / channel exit in S3, far field in S4)
- **Principle:** multi-grid RPA, same analyzer and position for all arms
- **References:** REF-LEMMER2025
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires W5 pre-registered ion-energy tolerance; error analysis per REF-LEMMER2025 (abstract only read - verify)
- **Calibration:**
  - grid transparency and potential calibration; collector current zero; time-averaged operation
- **Feasibility:** FEASIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE: recommended practice exists (REF-LEMMER2025); tolerance TBD

### INS-15. Faraday probes: source-exit collector, cold-transport I_del, far-field array

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M5, M8; **quantity:** I_src, I_del (Hall discharge off), far-field j(theta), beam current, divergence
- **Principle:** guarded Faraday probes; far field > 4 channel diameters; ion-saturation bias checked; SEE, gap and alignment corrections (REF-BROWN2017 Table A1 via lane 25)
- **References:** REF-BROWN2017
- **Required uncertainty:** I_del: enters the break-even screen (lane 25 S3), not R_arch; u(I_del) never creates a stop candidate<br>divergence: TBD - requires W5 pre-registered divergence tolerance; the full practice asks for >= 4 background pressures and 4 distances, the minimum uses fewer and says so (lane 25 M5)
- **Calibration:**
  - probe area measurement; bias sweep; CEX attenuation bound on the collector path (TBD - requires a cited N2+ on N2 charge-exchange cross section, package REQ-HW-05)
- **Feasibility:** AT_RISK: CEX bound on the collector path needs a cited cross section not yet in the repository

### INS-16. Langmuir probes (T_e, n_e), where feasible

- **Lane-25 measurements:** new (not in lane 25); **quantity:** T_e, n_e in the plume / interstage (not inside the Hall channel)
- **Principle:** single (or double) cylindrical probe; RF compensation in the rf_hall interstage (from memory - verify against REF-LOBBIA2017)
- **References:** REF-LOBBIA2017
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires a W5 decision on whether T_e/n_e is pre-registered; REF-LOBBIA2017 not read (metadata only)
- **Calibration:**
  - probe area; sweep calibration; RF-compensation check against a dummy-load pickup test
- **Feasibility:** AT_RISK: in-channel probing perturbs the discharge (from memory - verify); RF and microwave environments complicate interpretation; the owner lists T_e/n_e 'if feasible'

### INS-17. temperatures (stand, thruster, magnet circuit, sources, generators, cathode mount)

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M12; **quantity:** temperatures and settling
- **Principle:** thermocouples at fixed positions in every arm
- **References:** REF-POLK2017
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires T-SETTLE from S1 thermal time constants (lane 25)
- **Calibration:**
  - thermocouple calibration; stand thermocouples feed the thrust-drift model (REF-POLK2017)
- **Feasibility:** FEASIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE: standard

### INS-18. common time base and DAQ

- **Lane-25 measurements:** M4, M14; **quantity:** time stamps of every channel
- **Principle:** one time base for power, thrust, I_d(t), pressure and flow channels
- **Required uncertainty:** value: TBD - requires skew allowance from the S1 oscillation band
- **Calibration:**
  - timing check with a common trigger in S1a
- **Feasibility:** FEASIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE: standard
