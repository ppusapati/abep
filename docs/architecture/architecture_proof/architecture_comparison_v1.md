What this is not:
- not the A9.31 sec. 20 conclusion (the coordinator writes it after the decisive 196-state run)
- not a new physics model and not a new architecture search (A9.31 sec. 19)

## Common basis

- **RFP envelope** (`config/constraints/engineering_constraints_v1.json`, manifest `3a85581e…`): altitude 180-230 km; sustained thrust >= 12 mN; 25 mN capability; P_bus < 1500 W; wet mass < 40 kg; > 15,000 h firing; 26,280 h mission; atmospheric primary propellant with Xe capability; Hall preferred (RFP-P18-07, a preference).
- **Environment**: frozen `atmosphere_msis21_orbit_v1_design_states_v2` (196 states, `60073e214cf5…`).
- **Bus boundary**: `bus_power_boundary_a9_v2` for the active architecture. A9.30: bus_power_boundary_v1 is history and superseded for active flight closure where it carries cathode heater / keeper loads; the 1.5 kW limit is assessment-only.

Not used: 0-D Hall closure v1.2-v1.6: ABEP thrust, 2.5 kW closure, 110-120 kg, ECR+Hall P(success) 0.77 vs 0.63; every T, Isp, P_bus, T/D, MEV and life number of the v1.0 / v1.5 cross-family tables; v1.3 Hall hysteresis / low-mode collapse; screening candidates sgb-screen-01..09 (never members). ## Rejection / retention per alternative

- **ALT-B: RETAINED_SELECTED_INVESTIGATION_HYPOTHESIS** (OWNER_DECISION). Owner selection (A9, A9.19); not eliminated by any physical result in the repository. Its distinguishing weaknesses (neutralizer current capacity, RF power per ampere, maturity) are evidence gaps, not demonstrated infeasibility. Sources: `docs/decisions/OD_HARDWARE_PIVOT_2026_09_29_A9_hall_downstream_rf_icp_neutralizer.json`, `docs/decisions/OD_2026_10_01_A9_19_architecture_xe_contingency_owner_decision.json`, `docs/architecture/freeze_candidate/ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_CANDIDATE.md`.
- **ALT-A: NOT_PHYSICALLY_ELIMINATED; RETIRED_FROM_FLIGHT_BY_OWNER; GROUND_REFERENCE** (OWNER_DECISION supported by MECHANISM_LEVEL / PLANNING_ALLOCATION indicators). A9.19 removed the hollow cathode from the flight architecture and made Xe a contingency mode; A9.20 keeps C1 as ground reference. Preserved physical / architecture reasons that favour B over A: (1) continuous Xe consumption in air mode (5.4 kg at the 0.10 mg/s design target over 15,000 h; booked Xe terms exceed the 2 and 5 kg cases), (2) a thermionic emitter exposed to O-bearing back-flow at AO flux-equivalent pressures of the same order as the LaB6 O2 degradation range, with no AO data, (3) one more continuously used feed branch. None is verdict-bearing: lane-24 eliminated nothing and the veto layer marks every cell UNDETERMINED. A is a valid rejection only as an owner architecture decision, not as a physics veto. Sources: `docs/decisions/OD_2026_10_01_A9_19_architecture_xe_contingency_owner_decision.json`, `docs/decisions/OD_2026_10_01_A9_20_c1_ground_only_owner_decision.json`, `docs/architecture_comparison/veto_layer/VETO_LAYER.md`, `docs/architecture_comparison/cathode_integration/CATHODE_INTEGRATION.md`, `docs/budgets/mass_power_a9_v2/MASS_POWER_A9_V2.md`, `docs/architecture_comparison/hard_gates/hard_gate_status_v1.json`. What could reverse it: ALT-B's ICP fails its capacity / neutralization / life evidence (ICP-45, P1, HC-05) while a measured C1 minimum flow and an O-exposure tolerance make the cathode Xe and life affordable; a reversal needs a new owner decision (A9.19 is binding)..

What could reverse it: independent evidence of a nozzle energy channel beyond the energy-per-particle bound at ABEP flow.

Superseded for the primary campaign by A9 decision 3; break-even straddles; carries ALT-A's cathode and its Xe; the 0-D 'pre-ionisers add nothing' note is mechanism-level and is not confirmed by the later overlay.
