## 2. I_e,cap and the neutralization margin

| quantity | AIR_PRIMARY | XE_CONTINGENCY |
|---|---|---|

| GNG-ICP-01 | NOT_EVALUATED (criteria PENDING_OWNER_ACCEPTANCE) | same |

The Hall beam current is not available: HALL_NUMERICS_NOT_CONVERGED (A9.36). The **required** electron current is
therefore stated through registered bounds:

**Required electron current (B1/B2: CONSERVATION_BOUND, NOT_A_PERFORMANCE_PREDICTION).**
- The form is I_e,req ≥ I_d ≥ I_beam ≥ T (e / 2 m_i V_d,max)^½.
- It assumes singly charged ions, the heaviest ion of the mode, V_d,max = 350 V (DBF1-H1-05), no divergence, and
  neglects neutral thrust.

| | 12 mN | 25 mN |
|---|---|---|
| AIR (O₂⁺, 31.99954 u) | **≥ 0.788 A** | **≥ 1.641 A** |
| Xe (Xe⁺, 131.299 u) | **≥ 0.389 A** | **≥ 0.810 A** |

**Conservation capacity bound (B3/B4).**
- For the NP-ICP v2 model class, I_e,cap ≤ P_abs / E_iz,min. E_iz,min comes from the NIST evaluated ionization
  energies: NO 9.2642 eV, O₂ 12.0697 eV, Xe 12.1298 eV.
- At P_abs = 500 W the bound is 41.4–54.0 A (AIR) and 41.2 A (Xe). Conservation never excludes closure inside the
  frozen 500 W envelope.
- The minimum absorbed power is:
  - AIR: 7.3–9.5 W at 12 mN and 15.2–19.8 W at 25 mN;
  - Xe: 4.7 W at 12 mN and 9.8 W at 25 mN.

  In every case η_p,min ≤ 0.04 at P_net = 500 W.

**Published-analog risk indicator (M-C, Xe only; ANALOG_ESTIMATE_NOT_A_MODEL_RESULT; never changes the state).**
GK2008 sec. 4.5 gives an extraction cost of 230–450 eV per extracted ion for inductive (rf ion thruster) generators.

| Xe | P_abs needed | η_p needed at P_net 500 W | vs the anchor η_p 0.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 mN (≥ 0.389 A) | 89–175 W | 0.18–0.35 | ×1.8–3.5 short |
| 25 mN (≥ 0.810 A) | 186–365 W | 0.37–0.73 | ×3.7–7.3 short |

At the anchor coupling (η_p = 0.1, P_abs = 50 W) the analog gives 0.11–0.22 A, below the 12 mN lower bound.

**Engineering consequence (risk, not a verdict):** the RF → plasma power-transfer efficiency is the decisive ICP
design parameter. The Takahashi anchor value does not support the RFP thrust range inside the 500 W envelope. The P2
impedance map (R_p, R_ant in H-mode) and the P1 ICP-45 capacity measurement are the evidence that decides it. The
power ledger (P6) must also hold the ICP slot loads inside 1,350 W − P_common − P_Hall: at most 400 W at the
650 W lower Hall power band (information only).

**G-REUSE pressure information (B5).**
- With all feed isotropic in the tube at 300 K, p_ICP ≤ 0.0021 Pa (O) for the 12 mN conservation flow and ≤ 0.0091 Pa
  for the 25 mN flow (N₂ 0.0016 / 0.0069 Pa; Xe 0.0007 / 0.0032 Pa).
- The registered grid (0.001–0.1 Pa) covers these values and the Takahashi facility value (0.028 Pa).

## 5. What closes P4

1. **Rate sets.** Coordinator decision on the registry governance (sec. 6). Then:
   - Xe: GK2008 tables, the Xe set path and CA-12. XE-ION-03 and XE-REC-01 need bounds or an owner disposition.
   - AIR: the tier-1 tables, SB-NO / SB-He / SB-Ar, NEG-CRIT and CA-ICP-v1. Closed-access items go through the
     owner-approved acquisition route.
2. **AIR edge factor.** σ_i for O⁺ and O₂⁺.
3. **Delivered composition.** DCR-001 (P1) must register it.
4. **Hall demand.** I_d,max,H1 from a converged Hall envelope (P2 / A9.36) or an H-1 measurement.
5. **Coupling and validation.** The P2 impedance map (η_p, CM-CAL) and the P1 ICP-45 capacity bench give the
   VALIDATED_BENCH cell for HC-05.
6. **GNG-ICP-01.** The owner accepts its criteria.

## 6. Not changed in this lane, and why

- **The ICP chemistry registry.** Every tier-1 table build changes the codes that the frozen M2 record regenerates
  byte for byte. The P-ICP-DBF1 path reads the live registry, and the check is
  `crates/abep-assess/tests/m2_dbf1.rs::committed_m2_record_is_the_a8_record_of_today`. The M2 record is a frozen
  milestone record. Before any ICP table is built, the coordinator (M2-rerun owner) must decide between a registry
  snapshot pin for the M2 v1 regeneration and M2 v2. This record has the same "record of today" property: a registry
  change is a v2 of it.
- **Frozen records.** DBF-1, the M2 record, the NP-ICP and NP-ICP-CHEM-AIR preregistrations, and `hallthruster_bridge/`
  (including `propellants_air`, owned by L-HALL-CHEM-AIR) are not changed.
