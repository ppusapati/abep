## Governing supersession decisions

1. **Primary investigation hypothesis:** Hall accelerator/discharge followed by a **downstream 13.56 MHz RF inductively coupled plasma (ICP) electron source / neutralizer**. This is the Takahashi/Watanabe-type topology: RF is downstream, not an upstream pre-ionizer and not a parallel thrust branch.
2. **Control/fallback:** conventional Hall + heated Xe-fed LaB6 C1 remains the reference/control and fallback until the ICP neutralizer demonstrates electron-current capacity, beam neutralization, power, restart and life.
3. **Old upstream RF/ECR pre-ionizer campaign:** preserve all A5/prereg/evidence artifacts as historical. Do not delete or rewrite them, but do not spend new hardware/procurement effort on them as the primary campaign.
4. **Parallel RF||Hall v2:** keep the branch and A8 as a draft/historical alternative. Do not merge it into the primary line and do not invent missing inputs to make surfaces.
5. **New decision artifact:** create **A9 — Hall + downstream RF-ICP neutralizer investigation**, status `OWNER_AUTHORIZED_INVESTIGATION_HYPOTHESIS_NOT_FLIGHT_BASELINE`.
6. **First decisive hardware comparison:** keep the same H-1 Hall head, feed state, Vd/B settings, stand and metrology; compare the conventional C1 electron source against the downstream ICP electron source using swappable downstream modules and matched service-line/sham controls.
7. **Evidence sequence:** first reproduce the topology on Ar as **engineering-only** evidence; then N2; then O2-bearing atmospheric surrogate; separately use a dedicated atomic-O materials/life programme. No N2+O2 test is called an AO-life test.
8. **Requirement discipline:** 25 mN, <1.5 kW and <40 kg are treated as full-system gates. No discharge-only or RF-generator-only power claim is sufficient.

## External evidence anchors used for this decision pack

- Takahashi, Watanabe, Nakahama & Kikuchi (2024), *Hall thruster ion acceleration neutralized by a radiofrequency inductively coupled plasma*, Journal of Electric Propulsion 3:18, DOI 10.1007/s44205-024-00081-2.
- Current public DRDO/industry reporting describes the ABEP requirement as 180–230 km, 12–25 mN, <1500 W, <40 kg, ≥3-year mission, Hall preferred, atmospheric gas primary with Xe supplementary/alternative. Exact tender wording remains authoritative and must be verified from the official RFP.
- Existing repository governance, frozen validation results, A4–A7 decisions and P5-N2 v1/O4/S12 remain immutable.
