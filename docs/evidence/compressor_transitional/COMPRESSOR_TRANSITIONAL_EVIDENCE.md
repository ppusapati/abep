## Model-selection memo

Question: Which open-literature transitional-regime model of molecular-drag / turbomolecular compression can be implemented with every coefficient cited?

Criteria: full text openly and lawfully accessible; closed-form or reproducible relation with all coefficients stated; covers free molecular -> transitional (Kn dependence of compression and speed); states its own validation; reduces to the production free-molecular model in the overlap.

- **CT-S01 Skovorodko Eq. (1)**: SELECTED. only accessed model meeting all criteria: closed form, every factor stated, explicit continuum and free-molecular limits, reduces exactly to DragCompressor's linear Gaede characteristic in the free-molecular limit (with xi <-> sigma mapping).
  - limitation: author calls it approximate/qualitative
  - limitation: sigma is a fitting parameter (no a-priori value)
  - limitation: plane channel: no groove side walls, land leakage, curvature, centrifugal effects
  - limitation: inertia neglected, isothermal
  - limitation: single validation dataset (helium) not reconstructible
  - limitation: single gas; not a mixture model
- **CT-S02/CT-S03 Sharipov kinetic Holweck model**: NOT_IMPLEMENTABLE_NOW. best physics basis (linearized kinetic equation, experimentally compared), but the groove-flow solutions (Couette/Poiseuille coefficients vs rarefaction and groove shape) are in the non-open paper.
- **CT-S05 survey: Helmer-Levi, Sawada, Giors slip CFD**: NOT_IMPLEMENTABLE_NOW. primary papers not open; secondary equation incomplete.
- **CT-S04/CT-S08 TMP DSMC**: GAP. no open coefficients; see gap memo.

Result: Skovorodko Eq. (1) implemented as CANDIDATE_NOT_ADMITTED for drag-channel stages only.
