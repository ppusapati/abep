# Scaling / similarity audit: P5 evidence → Vyovrinda 12–25 mN Hall class (v1)

Generated by `scripts/architecture/scaling_similarity.py` (deterministic; `--check` verifies this file and `scaling_similarity.json`). Do not edit by hand.

**What this is.** It computes the dimensionless and similarity groups for P5 at its N₂ and Xe points and for a Vyovrinda 12–25 mN, < 1.5 kW analysis envelope on both RFP propellants (N₂ as the air proxy, and Xe). It shows which groups match or diverge and by how much, the dual-gas gap on one channel, which P5-derived conclusions are mechanism-level (likely transferable) and which are not, and what an admitted transport closure would need before it is applied to Vyovrinda geometry.

**What this is not.** It is not a design and not a performance prediction. It runs no Hall solver, claims no closure transfer, and ranks or eliminates no architecture. The Vyovrinda numbers are an **analysis envelope** built from sourced, xenon-derived scaling relations. The credible transport set is empty (gate 3 FAIL), so nothing here depends on a Hall transport closure. No screening candidate is used as a performance source; only their profile-coordinate convention is read, to show the geometry dependence. P5 calibration nuisance (depth 32/38 mm, coil shape) only widens the P5 reference ranges and is never a Vyovrinda option or axis.

Status: `ANALYSIS ONLY. No Hall solver run, no performance prediction, no closure transfer claimed, no architecture ranked or eliminated. Vyovrinda numbers are an analysis ENVELOPE from sourced (xenon-derived) scaling relations, not a design.`

Code baseline: `c53b75a`. Sources accessed 2026-09-26. Architectures: `hall_only`, `rf_hall`, `ecr_hall`. The groups describe the downstream Hall accelerator, which is common to all three arms. The rf_hall / ecr_hall arms change only the pre-ionization upstream of the Hall inlet; that acts on the ionization-length group through the inlet state, which the P5 evidence does not span (TR-16). No arm is ranked or eliminated.

## 2. Headline findings

1. **Gas-level ionization length (mechanism, transferable as a ratio).** At equal n_e, T_e and T_n the N₂ ionization mean free path is 16.6–16.7× the xenon one at T_e = 5 eV, 9.21–9.28× at 10 eV and 5.61–5.67× at 30 eV. N₂ ionization (all channels, including dissociative ionization) is slower and the neutral is faster. For N₂ the Melikov–Morozov criterion needs a correspondingly larger n_e·L. The ECHT extended channel (L/h = 8.6) is consistent with this (TR-01, TR-02).
2. **Same hardware, two gases (P5's own measured pair).** At equal T_e the P5-N₂ L/λ_i proxy is 0.0799–0.118× the P5-Xe value (T_e = 10 eV). To reach the Xe value at T_e(Xe) = 5 eV, N₂ needs T_e ≈ 13–16.1 eV; to reach it at T_e(Xe) = 10 eV, > 30 eV (outside the chemistry domain). So a channel that is Melikov–Morozov-similar on Xe is not on N₂ at the same T_e: N₂ needs a hotter discharge or a longer channel (TR-15). This is consistent with, but not evidence for, the P5-N₂ v1 out-of-domain pattern recorded in CLAUDE.md (solver T_e > 30 eV); no simulated T_e is used here.
3. **Thrust per power does not transfer.** P5-N₂ T/P_d is 18.7–19.9 mN/kW and the class-matched ECHT stable runs give 28.6–42.1 mN/kW. At 25 mN the N₂ envelope needs P_d = 1.25e+03–1.34e+03 W with P5-N₂-like conversion and 593–873 W with ECHT-like conversion; the Xe envelope with P5-Xe conversion needs 585–644 W. The RFP total bound is 1.5e+03 W. These spreads are transfer uncertainty (TR-06, TR-17), not predictions.
4. **Geometry is design-dependent and mostly smaller than P5.** The sourced sizing rules put the N₂-mode channel width at 5.13–26.1 mm and the Xe-mode width at 4.91–14.7 mm, against P5's 25 mm. Only the xenon critical-density rule applied to N₂ (a hypothesis, TR-14) reaches P5's width; every other rule gives h ≤ 17.1 mm. Surface-to-volume (1/h) and the wall heat-flux proxy rise accordingly (TR-10).
5. **Magnetic similarity cannot be kept on two criteria at once.** Keeping r_Le/L (B ∝ 1/L) and keeping the classical Hall parameter (B ∝ n_n) need B values that differ by factors of up to 5.15 across the N₂ envelope. The B *topology* is not captured by any scaling law (DM2011 p. 242). Any P5-identified anomalous-transport closure is therefore **not transferable** as is (TR-08).
6. **Verdict counts** (scenario × group; bands PROPOSED): N2: DESIGN_DEPENDENT 44, DIVERGES 1, NOT_COMPARABLE 4, SET_BY_CONSTRUCTION 31; Xe: DESIGN_DEPENDENT 44, DIVERGES 1, NOT_COMPARABLE 4, SET_BY_CONSTRUCTION 31. SET_BY_CONSTRUCTION marks groups the envelope inherits from its anchor (no evidence of similarity). A divergence is a transfer-risk flag, not a verdict on any architecture.
7. **Absolute proxies are not gates.** The exit-plane L/λ_i proxy for P5-N₂ never reaches GK's 95 % criterion (L/λ_i ≥ 3) at T_e ≤ 30 eV (max 1.14), although P5-N₂ sustained a discharge. The groups are therefore used only as ratios to the evidence (AR-5).

The plasma-parameter groups are **model-derived** and use no measured channel T_e or n_e. n_e is the exit-plane continuity estimate I_d/(e v_i A): I_d in place of I_i biases it high, the full-voltage v_i biases it low, and it understates the ionization-zone density (ions are slower there), so absolute L/λ_i values are order-of-magnitude only (section 5, proxy diagnostic). T_e is an assumed analysis grid. Comparisons of the same gas at the same T_e cancel the cross-section dependence. L is the physical channel length in every configuration (GK's L is the magnetized plasma thickness, about half the channel depth in its schematic).

Limitations and open questions:

- The scaling relations and coefficients (C_P*, j_d, n_n,c, f_loss, α range) come from xenon databases. Applying them to N₂ is extrapolation (evidence level 6). O/O₂/air are not covered (TBD: requires an O/O₂ reaction set and evidence).
- DM2008 Eq. (2.3) and DM2011 Eq. (3) write the ionization frequency with n_n; GK2008 Eq. (7.2-14) uses n_e, the density a neutral crossing a plasma actually meets. This audit uses the GK2008 form. The discrepancy is recorded, not resolved (DM2008's β = λ_i/L = 0.007 uses the n_n form).
- n_e and T_e are not measured for any configuration used here. Absolute L/λ_i values are order-of-magnitude, model-derived estimates; ratios between configurations are more robust.
- P5 I_d is the facility value P_d/V_d; thrust is the vacuum-corrected value. The N₂ I_d vacuum correction (section 3) is a few per cent, below the resolution of any verdict here.
- The ECHT radii (reading A), its B at the run coil currents and its thrust reduction are uncertain (audit). ECHT is a context anchor, not validation.
- The P5 channel depth (32 vs 38 mm) and coil shape are layer-1 nuisance. They appear only as brackets on P5 values and never as Vyovrinda variables.
- Related contracts are referenced by path only (never imported): `docs/hallmap/`, `docs/architecture_comparison/hall_reference/`, `abep_sim/arch_boundary.py`, `abep_sim/arch_compare.py`, `abep_sim/thermal_life.py`, `docs/evidence/hall_sustainment/`, `docs/evidence/wall_life/`, `docs/evidence/rf_source/`, `docs/evidence/ecr_source/`, `docs/evidence/cathode/`, `docs/architecture_comparison/experiment_protocol/`, `schemas/interfaces/`, `schemas/ledgers/`.
