Requirement text:

- **RFQ-06-R01** Procure/build a flight-representative breadboard discharge supply so eta_d and transient behaviour are measured before LOCK-2.
- **RFQ-06-R02** Input from the regulated internal propulsion bus of the breadboard/PPU architecture (not an RFP spacecraft interface requirement); spacecraft-input front end configurable. Supersedes the 28 V-class input in H2-4 H3-PPU-05 for A9 (flag to A9-07).
- **RFQ-06-R03** Rated to the relaxed 350 V end plus transient/qualification margin; lower end and power rating per the registered H-1 envelope.
- **RFQ-06-R04** Output power and current ratings follow the Hall discharge load and I_d,max of the registered H-1 envelope; the H2-4 PROPOSED '<= 1 kW' is context only.
- **RFQ-06-R05** The supply and its sense lines support the controlled quantity V_d = V_anode - V_electron-source-reference (not merely the terminal setting); terminal voltage and loop drops recorded as secondary quantities.
- **RFQ-06-R06** Laboratory discharge supply per H2-4 H3-PPU-01 (floating output, programmable current limit and ramp, arc/extinction recovery) - GROUND/FACILITY-ONLY.
- **RFQ-06-R07** The measurement chain computes P_bus,1ms,max = max over t of the 1 ms sliding mean of P_bus at the spacecraft-DC propulsion boundary; requirement < 1500 W for start-up and steady state unless the official RFP later states a transient exception.
- **RFQ-06-R08** Effective measurement bandwidth of every relevant channel.
- **RFQ-06-R09** Per relevant channel, or an equivalent direct spacecraft-bus power channel.
- **RFQ-06-R10** Arithmetic consequences of R08/R09.
- **RFQ-06-R11** All required channels synchronized on a common time base; anti-alias filtering documented; no step-average substituted for the 1 ms gate; the unaveraged sampled peak recorded separately (protection analysis only); 100 ms and 1 s averages reported as diagnostics.
- **RFQ-06-R12** One synchronized V and I channel pair per INSTALLED A9 bus slot of the configuration under test (VARIANT_ONLY slots as options); 4-wire voltage sensing, calibrated shunts or zero-flux transducers; floating-rated channels where the slot floats (collector/bias).
- **RFQ-06-R13** Supplier states DC V, I and power uncertainty per range with coverage factor.
- **RFQ-06-R14** The chain records heater power at every start-up step; until measured, the bus ledger books a TBD heater as ON at conservative/worst-case power (never a PASS by assuming it off).
- **RFQ-06-R15** Exploratory discharge-current chain to ~60 MHz if feasible; otherwise the measured bandwidth and its anti-alias/transfer function are declared.
- **RFQ-06-R16** Regulated internal-bus supply with a configurable spacecraft-input front end; front-end efficiency measured.
