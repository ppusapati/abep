Requirement text:

- **RFQ-02-R01** Thermal mass-flow controllers calibrated on their own gas (no gas-correction-factor or property-library conversion as the primary score-bearing standard); mixtures are verified through a traceable transfer / rate-of-rise method.
- **RFQ-02-R02** Four overlapping full-scale ranges per pure-gas path (N2, O2, Ar; see OQ-RFQ-02 for Ar).
- **RFQ-02-R03** Four ranges together cover this envelope (PRELIMINARY, model-derived in H2-6); the owner's H-1 operability/thermal characterization span is ~0.38-~3.2 mg/s delivered atmospheric flow (row 73).
- **RFQ-02-R04** Four ranges together cover this envelope (PRELIMINARY, model-derived in H2-6).
- **RFQ-02-R05** Arithmetic check that four geometric ranges can hold every point at or above the PROPOSED 20 % FS floor (CD-P-MFC-MINFRAC, not owner-given).
- **RFQ-02-R06** Ar-specific MFC calibration is required for HI-AR engineering interpretation; Ar remains engineering-only. Range TBD.
- **RFQ-02-R07** Xe reference/health check at the start of each installation (before N2 and any O2-bearing exposure); range TBD.
- **RFQ-02-R08** One high-accuracy C1 steady-flow controller of this class.
- **RFQ-02-R09** Readout and setpoint resolution at or below this value; digital setpoint.
- **RFQ-02-R10** The controller must realize the 0.005 mg/s search step (10 resolution steps per search step).
- **RFQ-02-R11** Separate start/diode-flow controller covering this range, if the 0.6-0.8 mg/s start flow is retained (OQ-RFQ-04).
- **RFQ-02-R12** Conversion for the supplier; the supplier names its own reference conditions and sccm convention (real/ideal gas, reference temperature).
- **RFQ-02-R13** The conservative +/-2 % FS class is the Xe-ledger basis until S1a demonstrates a better calibrated class on Xe; the supplier states its Xe-calibrated accuracy at the C1 range (the RFQ does not accept a GCF-derived Xe statement).
- **RFQ-02-R14** Each device supports an installed zero check per block (zero command/readback with upstream isolation) and states its body-temperature band and zero/span temperature coefficients; a body temperature output or a TC mounting point is provided.
- **RFQ-02-R15** O2-wetted controllers and lines cleaned to ASTM G93 Level C; no silver in O2/AO-wetted parts; wetted materials and seals declared.
- **RFQ-02-R16** Primary ICP gas mode is G-REUSE (no dedicated ICP feed, m_dot_ICP,dedicated = 0); a controller for the capped dedicated port is an OPTIONAL line item for the G-ATM / G-XE contingency variants only; no dedicated Xe to make the ICP work.
- **RFQ-02-R17** Vacuum-rated outlet, metal seals preferred, helium leak specification stated; attitude sensitivity stated (installation orientation of the test).
- **RFQ-02-R18** Each controller is delivered with a NABL / ISO-17025 calibration (primary score-bearing reference); the in-house rate-of-rise system is only a transfer/verification standard.

## Acceptance

- own-gas calibration certificate per device and per gas at the stated reference conditions (row 124, row 126)

- S1a verification against the in-house rate-of-rise transfer standard (row 126; UB-F-07)

## Calibration and traceability

- NABL / ISO-17025 calibration is the primary score-bearing flow reference; the in-house rate-of-rise system is a transfer/verification standard only (row 126; UB-F-06, UB-F-07)
- certificate states the distribution/coverage factor; a bare +/-x bound is treated as rectangular (UBQ-03)
- Ar calibration specific to Ar (UBQ-08); Xe calibration on Xe (H2-2 H3-C1-02)
