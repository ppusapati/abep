Requirement text:

- **RFQ-03-R01** Approximately 200 amu; 300 amu is not required unless later diagnostics require heavier species.
- **RFQ-03-R02** Differentially pumped sampling so the analyser operates within its pressure limit at the facility base pressure and at two elevated background-pressure levels.
- **RFQ-03-R03** Inlet pressure span of the sampling system.
- **RFQ-03-R04** Species calibration for N2, O2, O-related fragments and Xe; Ar-specific calibration for HI-AR engineering interpretation; supplier states the calibration method and the gas sensitivity factors basis.
- **RFQ-03-R05** Supplier states filament/ion-source material and its behaviour in O2-bearing gas (the O2-bearing stage is labelled NO_ATOMIC_O).
- **RFQ-03-R06** Chamber sampling point and a near-C1 sampling point option (INS-22 analog).
- **RFQ-03-R07** Supplier states detector options and minimum detectable partial pressure per species at the sampling inlet.

## Acceptance

- species calibration demonstrated for N2, O2, O-fragments, Xe and Ar (row 127, UBQ-08)
