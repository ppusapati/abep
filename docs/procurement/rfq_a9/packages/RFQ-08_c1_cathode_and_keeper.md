Requirement text:

- **RFQ-08-R01** Heated Xe-fed LaB6 hollow cathode as the conventional reference/control/fallback (not the primary flight neutralizer if the ICP succeeds), sized to the measured/derived current demand; quote unit + spare.
- **RFQ-08-R02** Emission range covering the registered discharge current demand plus keeper (not invented here).
- **RFQ-08-R03** External C1 on the C1 reference module seated on KC-1 (H-1 mean diameter not constrained).
- **RFQ-08-R04** Current-limited pulsed keeper ignition capability in the 300-600 V class with interlocks and recorded pulse energy.
- **RFQ-08-R05** Keeper lead, feedthrough, connectors, harness and their isolation to cathode common / module body / facility ground: upper operating pulse 600 V, minimum design isolation basis 900 V (1.5x the upper operating pulse). Flight level may be revised upward only, never downward after test results without controlled justification.
- **RFQ-08-R06** Qualification/hipot 1.0 kV DC at representative pressure/gas for the initial H-1/C1 development hardware; no flashover/breakdown; leakage recorded; separate actual 600 V pulse-waveform test. Does not replace the ICP-44 RF insulation qualification.
- **RFQ-08-R07** Current-regulated heater supply; heater command/power stated at every start-up step; until measured the bus ledger books the heater ON at conservative/worst-case power.
- **RFQ-08-R08** Vendor/design-qualified purge and ignition flow; each ignition dwell capped at 120 s with at most two retries (preliminary); all Xe booked.
- **RFQ-08-R09** Supplier states keeper material; graphite is not the flight baseline for an O/AO-exposed keeper; quote oxygen-resistant or ceramic-shielded alternatives for biased coupon screening.
- **RFQ-08-R10** Mandatory cathode-tube thermocouple point and a pyrometer view of the emitter where line of sight exists (tube temperature never relabelled as emitter temperature); the O-bearing experimental emitter floor is 1843 K (not a demonstrated lifetime solution).
- **RFQ-08-R11** Isolated, selectable cathode-common/bleeder topology with voltage and current measurement; no resistor value frozen.
- **RFQ-08-R12** Voltage rating of the cathode-branch dielectric break.

## Acceptance
