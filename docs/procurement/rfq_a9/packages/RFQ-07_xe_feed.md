Requirement text:

- **RFQ-07-R01** Size at the stated maximum storage temperature of 323 K using a verified Xe EOS and MEOP/safety factors; room-temperature density is not a sizing basis.
- **RFQ-07-R02** Quote per case (no single mission Xe load is frozen).
- **RFQ-07-R03** Arithmetic from the NIST reference EOS values recorded in R6; propellant only (excludes residual, reserve, ullage, MEOP margin).
- **RFQ-07-R04** Tank internal volume, MEOP and safety factors per case.
- **RFQ-07-R05** Two independent isolation valves in series on the flight high-pressure Xe/cathode branch (limited redundancy: dual series isolation plus critical sensing/FDIR).
- **RFQ-07-R06** Controllable flow covering the C1 steady range (0.05-0.2 mg/s class) and, if retained, start/diode flows 0.6-0.8 mg/s, plus the bounded Xe reference flows; all Xe booked (purge, preheat, ignition, keeper, transition, fallback).
- **RFQ-07-R07** The primary G-REUSE ICP mode has m_Xe,ICP = 0; no Xe feed is specified for the ICP; a G-XE contingency is a separate declared variant booked in the Xe ledger.
- **RFQ-07-R08** Include the filter/getter with an explicit <= 17 W-class load only after vendor/spec verification; account mass and pressure drop; not required for an ICP-only flight branch.
- **RFQ-07-R09** The primary A9 flight architecture does not carry C1 and the ICP together; C1 Xe hardware quoted here serves the ground comparison/control/fallback architecture.
- **RFQ-07-R10** Owner v0 dry allocation for Xe hardware (allocation, not a CBE; A9 recorder flag: below the H2-7 analog range); the 40 kg gate is wet (Xe + tank included).
- **RFQ-07-R11** Xe-service cleanliness class and wetted materials declared; metal seals.
